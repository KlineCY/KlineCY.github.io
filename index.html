01 QUANTITATIVE FINANCE

CTA多资产多空策略

CTA Multi-Asset Long-Short Strategy

围绕六类资产的37只ETF，构建动量、趋势与波动率等六类特征，并在逐月滚动框架中比较七类预测模型。研究以验证集表现选择Ridge模型，结合Ledoit–Wolf协方差估计与考虑风险、换手和敞口约束的组合优化方法形成多空仓位。

A systematic framework covering 37 ETFs across six asset classes. It combines six feature groups, monthly walk-forward evaluation of seven predictive models, validation-based selection of Ridge, Ledoit–Wolf covariance estimation, and constrained portfolio optimization.
