多期Brinson归因：几何主动收益与Carino链接

系统化研究与实验　完整源码

经济机制与模型设定

Brinson–Fachler分解将一期主动收益归为配置、选择与交互效应。多期直接累加各期效应不等于复利后的累计主动收益；Carino以对数收益差的斜率链接各期贡献，使累计归因与几何净值差一致。

推导与计算方法

每期按资产组汇总组合及基准收益，要求两套权重各自和1。计算单期三效应及对数差商，累乘组合和基准财富后求全期差商。主动收益接近零时采用稳定log1p差商，完全相等时使用导数极限。

$$A_{gt}=(w^P_{gt}-w^B_{gt})(r^B_{gt}-R^B_t)$$
$$S_{gt}=w^B_{gt}(r^P_{gt}-r^B_{gt}),\quad I_{gt}=(w^P_{gt}-w^B_{gt})(r^P_{gt}-r^B_{gt})$$
$$k_t=\frac{\log(1+R^P_t)-\log(1+R^B_t)}{R^P_t-R^B_t}$$
$$K=\frac{\log(1+R^P)-\log(1+R^B)}{R^P-R^B},\quad E^{\rm linked}_{gt}=(k_t/K)E_{gt}$$

可复算样本与解析基准

第一期组合收益0.06、基准0.05，两个组配置各0.003、选择0.01及−0.01、交互各0.002。第二期双方均0.03，三效应为零；累计主动收益为0.0103，链接后的贡献总和必须等于此值。

研究边界及计算复杂度

O(n)聚合加排序，期与组编号整数，拒绝重复组及负权重，组收益不小于−100%。归因解释依赖分类、基准和期初权重口径；不是因果效应。

C++17完整实现

inline Report portfolio_brinson_linked(const Mat& x,const Vec& p){
    validate(x,2,6,6);parameters(p,0);struct Period{double wp=0,wb=0,rp=0,rb=0;Mat rows;};std::map<double,Period> periods;std::set<std::pair<double,double>> seen;for(auto&r:x){require(r[0]==std::floor(r[0])&&r[1]==std::floor(r[1])&&r[2]>=0&&r[3]>=0&&r[4]>=-1&&r[5]>=-1,"Invalid attribution row");require(seen.insert({r[0],r[1]}).second,"Duplicate group period");auto&v=periods[r[0]];v.wp+=r[2];v.wb+=r[3];v.rp+=r[2]*r[4];v.rb+=r[3]*r[5];v.rows.push_back(r);}auto slope=[](double a,double b){double d=a-b;return d==0?1/positive(1+b):std::log1p(d/positive(1+b))/d;};double wp=1,wb=1;for(auto&kv:periods){auto&v=kv.second;require(std::abs(v.wp-1)<=1e-10&&std::abs(v.wb-1)<=1e-10,"Weights must sum to one");wp=positive(wp*(1+v.rp));wb=positive(wb*(1+v.rb));}double K=positive(slope(wp-1,wb-1)),active=wp-wb,s=0;Report o{{"Period","Group","Allocation","Selection","Interaction","LinkFactor","LinkedAllocation","LinkedSelection","LinkedInteraction","TotalActive","Residual"},{}};for(auto&kv:periods){auto&v=kv.second;double k=slope(v.rp,v.rb)/K;for(auto&r:v.rows){double a=(r[2]-r[3])*(r[5]-v.rb),b=r[3]*(r[4]-r[5]),c=(r[2]-r[3])*(r[4]-r[5]);s+=k*(a+b+c);o.rows.push_back({r[0],r[1],a,b,c,k,k*a,k*b,k*c,active,0});}}for(auto&r:o.rows)r.back()=std::abs(s-active);return o;
}
